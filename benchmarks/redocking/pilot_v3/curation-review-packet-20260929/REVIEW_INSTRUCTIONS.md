# Independent redocking eligibility review

This packet contains six raw structure files sampled with seed 20260929: three selected cohort cases and three rejected polymer-entity candidates. Labels were randomized; this packet does not reveal the curation decision or the rejection reasons.

Use ELIGIBILITY_CRITERIA.md and the target_entity_id in review_form.csv to assess each case independently. Record an independent verdict, the criteria confirmed or failed, and a concise rationale. Return the completed form and reviewer identity/date. An uncertain verdict is acceptable if it states what evidence is needed. Review eligibility only; do not judge docking outcomes or preparation suitability.

Do not consult the cohort manifest, complete candidate decision log, or the separate unblinding key until the review is signed and returned.
