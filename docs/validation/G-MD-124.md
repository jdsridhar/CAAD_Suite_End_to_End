# G-MD-124 — Bounded thermal equilibration and cross-engine energy evaluation for 1Z6E/IK8

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Scope:** Bounded NVT heating, NPT density relaxation, and matched Amber-versus-GROMACS single-point potential energy evaluation on independently equilibrated configurations of 1Z6E/IK8 across both Form 0 (neutral) and Form +1 (cationic) microstates. Characterizes cross-engine behavior; does not qualify Amber/GROMACS compatibility or claim production equilibrium.

## 1. Bounded Thermal Equilibration Protocol

Following the two-stage minimization in G-MD-123, both systems underwent:
1. **NVT Heating Stage:**
   - 10 ps (5,000 steps, $dt = 0.002\text{ ps}$), linear heating from 100.0 K to 303.15 K.
   - Langevin thermostat ($\gamma_{\text{ln}} = 2.0\text{ ps}^{-1}$), SHAKE on all hydrogen bonds ($ntc = 2, ntf = 2$), 10.0 A nonbonded cutoff, PME electrostatics.
   - Positional restraint: 5.0 $\text{kcal/mol/\AA}^2$ on solute heavy atoms (`:1-287 & !@H=`).
   - Seed: 44101.
2. **NPT Density Relaxation Stage:**
   - 20 ps (10,000 steps, $dt = 0.002\text{ ps}$), target $T = 303.15\text{ K}$, target $P = 1.0\text{ bar}$.
   - Berendsen barostat ($\tau_p = 2.0\text{ ps}$, isotropic coupling), Langevin thermostat.
   - Positional restraint: 2.0 $\text{kcal/mol/\AA}^2$ on solute heavy atoms.
   - Seed: 44102.

### Residual Context & Interpretation
- On this ~35,600-atom solvated system with ~10,000 water molecules, the net delta of $\sim +570$ to $+597\text{ kcal/mol}$ corresponds to an average difference of $\sim 0.016\text{ kcal/mol}$ ($\sim 0.067\text{ kJ/mol}$) per atom.
- This is fully consistent with earlier findings across G-MD-44 through G-MD-112, where differences in PME short-range evaluation (Verlet pair-list buffer, Ewald grid distribution, and analytical versus table lookup modes) accumulate across macroscopic aqueous lattices.
- The relative potential energy difference remains bounded at $\le 0.58\%$.
