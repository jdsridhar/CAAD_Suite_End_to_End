# G-MD-118 — Independent candidate preflight: 1Z6E/IK8

**Disposition:** Select 1Z6E/IK8 for an Amber preparation trial, not as an MD-ready or Amber/GROMACS-qualified system. The deposited crystallographic model is chemically distinct from 4W52/benzene and 5NIU/RC8 and has useful ligand-site coverage. Preparation must explicitly preserve the biological assembly's inter-chain disulfide, inspect partial-occupancy residues, resolve incomplete side chains, and select a pH-appropriate ligand microstate before parameterization.

## Structure and component audit

RCSB identifies 1Z6E as a human factor Xa inhibitor complex solved by X-ray diffraction at 1.80 A. Its unique ligand component is IK8 (C24H20F4N8O2). The asymmetric/biological assembly includes a 234-residue protease chain and a 52-residue factor-X domain chain; RCSB reports a hetero 2-mer. This is an independent target from the previously assessed systems. See the [RCSB 1Z6E entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1Z6E) and its [wwPDB validation report](https://files.rcsb.org/validation/view/1z6e_full_validation.pdf).

Read-only parsing of the downloaded mmCIF with Gemmi found:

- Chain A: 234 polymer residues, 123 crystallographic waters, and the IK8 ligand (38 heavy atoms).
- Chain L: 52 polymer residues and 38 crystallographic waters.
- Eight deposited disulfide connections, including one inter-chain bond between chain A CYS122 and chain L CYS132 (2.547 A). Retain both chains and verify that topology construction preserves this bond.
- No metal or other non-water small-molecule component was present in the parsed model.
- Fourteen unobserved atoms are recorded in three residues: LYS62 (CG/CD/CE/NZ), ARG150 (CG/CD/NE/CZ/NH1/NH2), and LYS223 (CG/CD/CE/NZ). The nearest observed atom of ARG150 is 8.47 A from the ligand; do not silently treat its missing side chain as a complete observed structure.
- No alternate-location IDs were present for the parsed heavy atoms. Several residues have atom occupancies below 0.99, including ARG150; inspect these before choosing modeled coordinates.
- The closest ligand–protein heavy-atom pair is IK8 N1 to ASP189 OD2 at 2.670 A. There are zero heavy-atom pairs below 2.5 A and two below 3.0 A in the deposited coordinates. This is a geometry screen, not a hydrogen-bond or protonation assignment.

The Chemical Component Dictionary ideal SDF contains 58 atoms (38 heavy atoms plus 20 hydrogens) and formal charge zero. That neutral CCD representation is not, by itself, a pH 7.4 ligand state. Run the configured microstate/protonation workflow and retain its atom mapping; if the platform reports an ambiguous state, pause for the documented human decision rather than parameterizing an arbitrary form.

## Gate status and next actions

This preflight passes only the candidate-selection screen. It does not verify missing-sidechain reconstruction, ligand parameter coverage/quality, protonation, solvent/ion model compatibility, minimization, or MD readiness. The next gate is a reproducible AmberTools build using the selected biological assembly, with explicit treatment of the eight disulfides and all three incomplete residues. Inspect the built complex and topology, then perform the established independent zero-cycle and restrained-minimization checks. Do not start production or sampling MD unless those checks pass.
