### Decision Policy Under ADR-0014
Under ADR-0014 sec 2:
- When more than one plausible protonation state is predicted within the physiological window, automatic selection is prohibited, and a `DECISION_REQUIRED` event is triggered.
- Permitted resolutions:
  1. Select Form +1 (physiologically predominant cationic state).
  2. Select Form 0 (neutral parent).
  3. Branch and evaluate both forms as independent candidate forms of the parent compound.
