# G-MD-108 — Localizing the 5NIU/RC8 electrostatic energy residual

**Result:** Reparsed the retained Sander CLI and GROMACS energy-term outputs for all 15 matched 5NIU/RC8 frames from the three G-MD-44 replicas. The mean total GROMACS-minus-Amber electrostatic difference was −2.44790 kcal/mol. The GROMACS Coulomb-14 minus Amber 1-4 EEL contribution was a small **positive** +0.18960 kcal/mol. The remaining non-1-4 electrostatic comparison (GROMACS Coulomb SR + reciprocal minus Amber EEL) averaged **−2.63750 kcal/mol**, accounting for and exceeding the net residual. This localizes the discrepancy to the aggregate non-1-4 electrostatic calculation; it does not separate direct-space from reciprocal-space causes or establish engine incompatibility.

## Method

Used the 15 previously retained coordinate-matched frames at 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500 ps in each of three replicas. Amber EEL and 1-4 EEL were parsed separately from the original Sander CLI FINAL RESULTS sections. GROMACS Coulomb-14, Coulomb (SR), and Coul. recip. columns were parsed from each replica's energy-whole-terms.xvg, converted from kJ/mol to kcal/mol using 4.184. For each frame, the sum of the separately parsed terms was checked against the stored paired-energy electrostatic delta; all 15 matched within 1×10⁻⁵ kcal/mol.

At replica 1, 100 ps, GROMACS Coulomb-14 was 5564.75608 kcal/mol versus Amber 1-4 EEL 5564.5618 (+0.19428). GROMACS Coulomb SR + reciprocal summed to −70033.59002 kcal/mol versus Amber EEL −70030.9550 (−2.63502). Their sum reproduces the stored total electrostatic difference of −2.44074 kcal/mol.

The three groupings are bookkeeping comparisons, not independent observations. All snapshots are correlated, and all belong to one 5NIU/RC8 topology and one converted parameter set. The means/ranges are descriptive only and do not provide confidence intervals or thresholds.

## Interpretation and next diagnostic

The 1-4 contribution is small and offsets the main residual by about +0.19 kcal/mol. The outstanding discrepancy sits in the combined non-1-4 electrostatics. GROMACS reports direct-space Coulomb SR and PME reciprocal energy separately; the retained Amber EEL value is a combined number. This analysis therefore cannot attribute the residual to real-space summation, PME reciprocal interpolation, exclusions/self terms, precision, or another convention. The ~0.025 kcal/mol Sander API/CLI EEL discrepancy from G-MD-107 remains a separate smaller question.

Next compare same-coordinate term changes across controlled PME grids/precision and inspect available Amber Ewald diagnostics before testing independent systems. Do not use the present component grouping as proof of cause, a compatibility threshold, or a reason to enable the Amber/GROMACS profile. No new dynamics were run.
