# G-MD-109 — Amber verbose Ewald decomposition on 5NIU/RC8 frames

**Finding:** Running Amber Sander CLI with PME verbose diagnostics on the same 15 5NIU/RC8 restart frames split Amber's non-1-4 electrostatics into self, reciprocal, direct, and adjustment components. Grouping Amber direct + self + adjustment against GROMACS Coulomb (SR) gives a mean GROMACS-minus-Amber difference of **−2.48394 kcal/mol**. Comparing GROMACS Coul. recip. against Amber reciprocal gives **−0.15365 kcal/mol**. These groupings sum to the previously measured **−2.63759 kcal/mol** non-1-4 residual. The residual is therefore concentrated in the short-range grouping, but this does not prove the two grouped terms have identical definitions or identify the cause.

## Method

Sander CLI was run as an energy-only evaluation on each retained Amber restart using PME, 10 Å cutoff, vdwmeth=0, explicit 60×81×48 PME grid, and verbose=2. Input frames were not propagated. The four Amber Ewald values (self, rec, dir, adj) were extracted from each Sander output. GROMACS Coulomb-SR and Coulomb-reciprocal values were read from the matching saved whole-trajectory energy XVG and converted from kJ/mol to kcal/mol using 4.184. Existing paired coordinates are the same ones used to generate those Amber restart files. All 15 outputs completed and yielded the full decomposition.

## Results

| Diagnostic grouping, GROMACS minus Amber (kcal/mol) | Mean | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GROMACS Coulomb (SR) − (Amber direct + self + adjustment) | −2.48394 | −2.55804 | −2.40720 |
| GROMACS Coul. recip. − Amber reciprocal | −0.15365 | −0.19426 | −0.13431 |
| Sum / non-1-4 electrostatic difference | −2.63759 | −2.71833 | −2.55902 |

The sum matches G-MD-108's separately calculated non-1-4 residual, within output rounding. The paired 1-4 contribution remains about +0.18960 kcal/mol on average, leaving the net electrostatic residual about −2.44790 kcal/mol.

GROMACS documents Coulomb-SR as containing more than a bare direct-space pair sum, including reciprocal exclusion corrections and the Ewald self term. Amber's verbose direct/self/adjustment partition is not guaranteed to map term-for-term onto GROMACS's reported buckets. The comparison is a useful diagnostic grouping, not a derivation of identical energy definitions. G-MD-79's independent pair-sum study also found significant CPU PP thread-count sensitivity on these coordinates; that remains a possible numerical contributor, but this experiment does not assign causality.

## Limits

Only one parameterized chemical system and three correlated 500 ps replicas were sampled. PME settings and coordinates are matched to the retained G-MD-44 comparison, but the values do not establish reproducibility across builds, systems, or hardware. No statistical uncertainty, tolerance, compatibility profile, or force-field qualification follows. No MD was run.

Next compare against an independent direct-space/Ewald reference under the same coordinates and assess whether controlled changes in CPU nonbonded accumulation or PME settings shift the dominant short-range-group residual. Then extend the Amber/Sander comparison to chemically distinct systems. Keep the compatibility profile disabled.
