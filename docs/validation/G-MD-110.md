# G-MD-110 — Ewald potential-modifier audit

**Finding:** The G-MD-79 independent pair-sum code subtracted the Ewald cutoff shift for included pairs, but its archived energy MDP/TPR explicitly says `coulomb-modifier = None`. This is a small input-model mismatch in the reference definition. Removing that included-pair term changes the mean five-frame reference residual by only 0.094 kJ/mol; the CPU/reference residual remains about 5.74 kJ/mol (reference minus GROMACS). A same-coordinate GROMACS 2026.3 control changing only the requested modifier produced a mean Coulomb-SR difference of +0.131 kJ/mol (range −1.344 to +1.344). The modifier effect is unresolved because the generated `ld-seed` differed between TPRs; the measured deltas cannot be attributed to the modifier. A separately computed +39.249 kJ/mol excluded-pair charge-product constant is not a valid standalone correction and was not applied.

## Method

Used G-MD-79 GRO-rounded frames: replica 1 at 100/200/300/400 ps and replica 2 at 100 ps. Each frame/box was preprocessed from the same topology into two TPRs; the only requested MDP change was `coulomb-modifier=None` versus `Potential-shift`. Both MDPs left `ld-seed=-1`, so grompp generated different TPR seeds (for 300 ps: −482624033 vs −560007971), confounding the comparison. PME grid 60×81×48, ewald-rtol 9.9979e-5, Coulomb/vdW cutoffs 1.0 nm. CPU energy-only reruns used one MPI rank and four OpenMP threads. No dynamics were integrated.

The 27,365 topology exclusions have sum(qi*qj) = −2825.56148 e². Multiplying by the Ewald shift coefficient gives +39.24879 kJ/mol as an isolated algebraic value. That product is diagnostic only; it is not asserted to be a valid correction to GROMACS's complete direct/reciprocal exclusion expression.

Removing the included-pair shift from the G-MD-79 reference to match `None` gives mean reference-minus-GROMACS +5.743 kJ/mol (range +5.561 to +5.869). Thus the correction does not explain the observed CPU residual or thread-count pattern, but G-MD-79's exact formula-matching claim is narrowed.

## Limits

The five frames are correlated and from one system; GRO coordinates are rounded. Individual observed differences are on the scale of CPU accumulation variability, and auto-generated TPR `ld-seed` values differed, so this does not establish equivalence or the modifier's isolated physical effect. No tolerance, compatibility qualification, or trajectory claim follows. Continue with controlled accumulation/backend replication and chemically distinct systems.
