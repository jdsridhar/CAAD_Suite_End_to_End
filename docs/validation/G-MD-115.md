# G-MD-115 — 4W52 ASP9 CG gradient and restraint decomposition

**Correction (G-MD-116):** The central differences reported below were taken from Sander's EAMBER field, which excludes the positional-restraint contribution; they were not differences of the total potential. The analytic restraint gradient must therefore be **added**, not subtracted. The corrected total-gradient estimate is **(+5.385, +1.532, −1.423) kcal mol-1 A-1**, whose largest component agrees with Sander's reported GMAX 5.3884 within finite-difference precision. An independent Sander force-frame probe confirms the x component. The earlier inferred non-restraint norm of 7.148 and the claim of an unexplained GMAX inconsistency are withdrawn. See [G-MD-116](G-MD-116.md) for the energy-term and force evidence.

## Method and checks

Six zero-cycle Sander energy/force evaluations used the same topology, PME/cutoff, positional-restraint input and reference coordinates as G-MD-114. Only atom 174's x, y, or z coordinate was displaced by ±0.01 A from the saved checkpoint. Central differences were calculated from the printed EAMBER values (0.0001 kcal/mol display precision; worst-case energy-rounding contribution about 0.005 kcal mol-1 A-1 per derivative component). In this Sander output, EAMBER is the non-restraint potential; the separate RESTRAINT term is excluded from it. A preliminary ±0.001 A probe gave x/y/z components (+6.15, −0.05, −1.20); its raw outputs were overwritten during the larger-step rerun, so it is only a diagnostic cross-check, not retained primary data.

The checkpoint atom's displacement from the original reference is (−0.03777, +0.07986, −0.01342) A, norm 0.08936 A. The restrained atom selection contains 1,306 atoms. Recomputing the reported restraint energy as 10 × sum(Δx²) gives 140.584882 kcal/mol, matching Sander's printed 140.5849 kcal/mol; this validates the restraint-energy convention used to calculate the atomwise restraint gradient (2 × 10 × Δx). Add this restraint derivative to the finite-difference derivative of EAMBER to estimate the total gradient. See the [Amber22 Reference Manual](https://ambermd.org/doc12/Amber22.pdf) for the positional-restraint form and XMIN settings.

The three listed covalent distances from ASP9 CG to CB, OD1, and OD2 are 1.5245, 1.2552, and 1.2468 A. Its closest other-residue heavy-atom neighbor is ARG144 CG at 3.454 A under periodic minimum-image distance; the next listed neighbors are TYR160 OH at 3.577 A and MET5 O at 3.684 A. These measurements reveal no obvious severe local contact at this atom, but they do not rule out a broader structural or electrostatic issue.

## Interpretation and limits

This local analysis does not identify the source of the non-restraint gradient. Its nearest listed contacts and bonded distances do not show an obvious local clash. The restraint opposes part of the total x-gradient. The GMAX/finite-difference convention issue is resolved, but the minimization readiness criterion remains unmet (RMS gradient 0.061101 versus drms=1e-4); 4W52 remains unqualified for MD and Amber/GROMACS compatibility. No criterion was changed.
