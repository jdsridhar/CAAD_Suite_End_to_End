# G-MD-115 — 4W52 ASP9 CG gradient and restraint decomposition

**Finding:** At the saved 1,000-cycle XMIN checkpoint, atom 174 (CG, ASP9) has a substantial local gradient that is not explained by its positional restraint alone. Central finite differences of Sander's printed total potential at ±0.01 A gave gradient components **(+6.140, −0.065, −1.155) kcal mol-1 A-1**, norm **6.248**. The analytical positional-restraint gradient on this atom is (−0.755, +1.597, −0.268), norm **1.787**. Subtracting that restraint contribution leaves an estimated non-restraint gradient norm **7.148 kcal mol-1 A-1**. The finite-difference maximum component (6.140) does not match Sander's reported GMAX 5.3884 for the same checkpoint. This discrepancy is unresolved; neither value is used as an acceptance threshold.

## Method and checks

Six zero-cycle Sander energy/force evaluations used the same topology, PME/cutoff, positional-restraint input and reference coordinates as G-MD-114. Only atom 174's x, y, or z coordinate was displaced by ±0.01 A from the saved checkpoint. Central differences were calculated from the printed EAMBER values (0.0001 kcal/mol display precision; worst-case energy-rounding contribution about 0.005 kcal mol-1 A-1 per derivative component). A preliminary ±0.001 A probe gave x/y/z components (+6.15, −0.05, −1.20); its raw outputs were overwritten during the larger-step rerun, so it is only a diagnostic cross-check, not retained primary data.

The checkpoint atom's displacement from the original reference is (−0.03777, +0.07986, −0.01342) A, norm 0.08936 A. The restrained atom selection contains 1,306 atoms. Recomputing the reported restraint energy as 10 × sum(Δx²) gives 140.584882 kcal/mol, matching Sander's printed 140.5849 kcal/mol; this validates the restraint-energy convention used to calculate the atomwise restraint gradient (2 × 10 × Δx). See the [Amber22 Reference Manual](https://ambermd.org/doc12/Amber22.pdf) for the positional-restraint form and XMIN settings.

The three listed covalent distances from ASP9 CG to CB, OD1, and OD2 are 1.5245, 1.2552, and 1.2468 A. Its closest other-residue heavy-atom neighbor is ARG144 CG at 3.454 A under periodic minimum-image distance; the next listed neighbors are TYR160 OH at 3.577 A and MET5 O at 3.684 A. These measurements reveal no obvious severe local contact at this atom, but they do not rule out a broader structural or electrostatic issue.

## Interpretation and limits

This local analysis does not identify the source of the non-restraint gradient. Its nearest listed contacts and bonded distances do not show an obvious local clash, while the finite difference indicates the restraint is opposing part of the total gradient rather than causing all of it. Broader atom/component attribution and the Sander GMAX/finite-difference mismatch remain open. No minimization acceptance criterion was changed, no dynamics were run, and 4W52 remains unqualified for MD and Amber/GROMACS compatibility.
