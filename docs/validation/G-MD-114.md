# G-MD-114 — 4W52/benzene XMIN minimization diagnostic

**Status: inconclusive; MD readiness remains failed.** AmberTools Sander's XMIN/LBFGS optimizer was tried as an alternate to the prior steepest-descent/conjugate-gradient minimizations, holding the Amber topology, PME setup, cutoff, positional restraints, and original reference coordinates fixed. The scratch attempt was configured for 5,000 iterations and the original `drms=1e-4 kcal mol-1 A-1` criterion, but was stopped after 1,200 iterations because progress was slow and the recorded RMS gradient was not approaching the criterion. This is an interrupted optimizer diagnostic, not a completed minimization or a claim that XMIN cannot converge.

## Method

AmberTools 23.6 / Sander 22.0 read the existing 21,659-atom Amber `system.prmtop`. Input coordinates were the retained Sander minimization-4 restart; the positional-restraint reference remained the original builder `system.inpcrd`. The 10 kcal mol-1 A-2 restraint on protein heavy atoms (:1-164 & !@H=), periodic box, PME grid (60×64×72), order 4, 10 A cutoff and PME tolerance were unchanged. Only the minimizer was changed to `ntmin=3`; Sander's captured control output confirms `xmin_method=LBFGS`. The Amber reference manual documents XMIN under `ntmin=3` and identifies LBFGS as the default XMIN method ([Amber22 Reference Manual](https://ambermd.org/doc12/Amber22.pdf)).

A separate zero-cycle Sander energy/force calculation evaluated the restart saved at the 1,000-iteration output interval. This was necessary because Sander explicitly warns in the XMIN output that intermediate restart coordinates and printed intermediate energies need not correspond; the checkpoint was therefore evaluated directly rather than assigning it the 1,000-iteration mdout energy.

## Observations

- The XMIN output sampled every 100 iterations reports a lower restrained objective from −87,432.7125 kcal/mol at iteration 0 to −87,570.9719 kcal/mol at iteration 1,200.
- The printed RMS gradient fluctuated: 0.020265 at the prior minimizer endpoint, 0.054370 at iteration 1,000, 0.029157 at iteration 1,100, and 0.056456 kcal mol-1 A-1 at iteration 1,200. The 1e-4 target was not met.
- A zero-cycle evaluation of the saved iteration-1,000 restart returned −87,552.9642 kcal/mol, RMS gradient **0.061101 kcal mol-1 A-1**, and maximum gradient **5.3884 kcal mol-1 A-1** at atom 174, CG of ASP 9. ParmEd reports its bonded distances to CB/OD1/OD2 as 1.5245/1.2552/1.2468 A; these are descriptive distances and do not by themselves explain the gradient.
- The minimization process was stopped by operator interruption at iteration 1,200, before its configured 5,000-cycle cap. Its exit status was nonzero as expected for an interrupted process. No completed XMIN final result was produced.
- No dynamics were run, and the Amber/GROMACS compatibility profile remains disabled.

These results do not establish whether another minimizer configuration or scientifically justified structural/parameter resolution can converge. They show only that this partial XMIN continuation did not meet the unchanged convergence criterion and did not demonstrate improved readiness.
