# G-MD-121 — Protein occupancy policy and export validation

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Scope:** Generic PDBFixer worker behavior, normalized `PreparedReceptor/1.3` contract, active workflow references, and real PDBFixer/Meeko/Vina integration. This is a structure-format and software-path validation; it does not establish MD readiness or docking accuracy.

## Change under validation

The worker protocol is `caddsuite.pdbfixer-worker/5`. The default `require_full_occupancy` policy stops with a decision-required error when selected polymer atoms have non-unit occupancies. The explicit `highest_occupancy_single_model` policy selects a unique highest-mean-occupancy alternate conformer per residue; ties fail visibly. Zero-occupancy polymer records are removed before PDBFixer so absent side-chain atoms can be rebuilt. The selected single-model derivative is explicitly written with occupancy 1.0, while source observations and policy are retained in the worker result and `PreparedReceptor/1.3` metadata.

Read-back validation checks mmCIF and PDB occupancies and requires both output atom counts to match the OpenMM topology. mmCIF retains connection records; the PDB derivative does not preserve those records and remains a docking-format derivative, not an authoritative MD topology source.

## Real worker trials

### 1Z6E, chains A and B

PDBFixer 1.12.0, OpenMM 8.4, and Biopython 1.88 ran at pH 7.4 with seed 44101 and the explicit highest-occupancy policy. The worker observed 34 non-unit-occupancy source atoms, including 14 zero-occupancy side-chain atoms; those 14 were rebuilt. The output has 4,401 atoms across 286 residues, with no geometry contacts below the worker's 1.5 Å diagnostic threshold. Post-run inspection confirmed full occupancy in both output formats and all eight mmCIF connection/disulfide records retained. This does not resolve the source's partial-occupancy/non-rotameric pocket concerns, choose an IK8 protonation state, or qualify the structure for MD.

Scratch output SHA-256 values:

- Prepared mmCIF: `4c7777eb092bb42bb08cba83218c409610cbf03067f6476cc4f920aedd427df1`
- PDB derivative: `50732cca5f2f1ff96b8e48d7c176193c7e3788129f9155aa85b4907054364f15`

### 5NIU, chain A

The explicit policy selected alternate conformers for three residues (CYS label 23: A; GLU label 41: B; CYS label 97: A). It reported 21 source non-unit occupancy atoms and generated 2,019-atom outputs whose read-back occupancies are 1.0. The geometry diagnostic found a terminal HIS143 O–OXT separation of 0.5137 Å. That severe geometry finding remains unresolved and blocks any MD-readiness claim for this prepared structure. Meeko consumed the PDB derivative and produced receptor PDBQT and parameter JSON, confirming the docking-format route only.

## Disposition and limits

The generic occupancy/export defect is addressed and its explicit decision behavior is tested. This does not automatically make a chosen conformer scientifically correct: occupancy selection is a model choice, not recovery of an ensemble. The 5NIU terminal geometry anomaly is still a blocking structure-quality issue. For 1Z6E, pocket disorder, ligand microstate, parameterization, geometry/minimization, and the MD readiness gate remain outstanding. No production MD, MM/PBSA, or DFT calculation is claimed by this validation.
