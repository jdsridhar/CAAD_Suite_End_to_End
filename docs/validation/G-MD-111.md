# G-MD-111 — Reconcile Amber/GROMACS residual by backend and frame precision

**Finding:** The five-frame G-MD-110 CPU comparison and G-MD-109's 15-frame comparison used different GROMACS short-range backends. G-MD-109's retained logs show GPU short-range kernels with CPU PME; G-MD-79 reran the rounded frames with CPU short-range kernels. On the five matching replica/time frames, the full-precision GPU-PP grouped residual averages **−2.517 kcal/mol** (GROMACS minus Amber direct+self+adjust), while the GRO-rounded CPU-4 residual averages **−3.815 kcal/mol**. Their gap is **−1.298 kcal/mol**. A G-MD-79 same-GRO CPU-4 versus GPU-PP rerun gives **−1.346 kcal/mol** mean (CPU minus GPU). The backend shift therefore accounts for nearly all of the sample-mean gap; a residual **+0.047 kcal/mol** remains and may include coordinate/box rounding and other small differences. No causal claim beyond these measured comparisons.

## Method and frame identity

Matched replica 1 at 100, 200, 300, and 400 ps, plus replica 2 at 100 ps. The Amber restart coordinates from G-MD-109 and GRO-derived restarts from G-MD-79 match the same atom order and frame; periodic coordinate RMS after GRO rounding is about **0.0050 Å** for every pair, maximum atom displacement **0.0086 Å**. Box component differences are below **0.00005 Å**. The frame coordinates are thus the same trajectory snapshots to the expected GRO precision.

G-MD-109 GROMACS Coulomb-SR values came from the retained `energy-whole-rerun.edr`; all three replica logs state “Using GPU 8x4 nonbonded short-range kernels.” Its paired Amber restarts retain full precision. G-MD-79 reran the corresponding GRO frames on CPU PP/CPU PME with four OpenMP threads, and also ran GPU PP/CPU PME on the same rounded coordinates. Energies were energy-only reruns; no dynamics were advanced. Amber `direct+self+adjust` remains a diagnostic grouping against GROMACS Coulomb-SR.

The residual after subtracting the measured CPU/GPU backend difference from the between-analysis gap averages **+0.0474 kcal/mol**. The five-frame full-precision mean also closely matches G-MD-109's 15-frame mean of −2.48394 kcal/mol. This reconciles the principal difference between those reported means: backend selection explains most of it, while the five-frame CPU/GPU comparison and full-precision versus GRO-rounded comparison are not one fully controlled experiment.

## Limits and next control

Only five correlated snapshots from one system were crosswalked. CPU and GPU energy outputs on identical GRO frames directly quantify the backend term, but the full-precision GPU versus rounded CPU comparison changes backend and coordinate/box precision together. Follow up with CPU and GPU reruns on the same full-precision TRR frames, fixed TPR/settings and explicit thread counts, then compare to Sander on those exact frames. This remains diagnostic validation: no tolerance, Amber/GROMACS compatibility profile, or trajectory equivalence is established.
